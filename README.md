# FIT3179 Data Visualisation 2 — Australia's Digital Divide

A single-page Vega-Lite visualisation about digital government services and digital inclusion in Australia.

## Run locally

1. Open this folder in VS Code.
2. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.
3. The URL should begin with `http://127.0.0.1:5500/`.

## Structure

- `index.html` — full scrolling story and all chart embeds
- `style.css` — responsive styling and typography
- `data/` — chart CSV data
- `vega/` — 12 readable Vega-Lite specifications
- `docs/` — rubric audit, interview preparation and submission description
- `sketch.pdf` — visualiation design sketch of the assignment

## Story chapters

1. The digital shift
2. Experience matters
3. Place shapes participation
4. Distance compounds the gap
5. Who is left behind?

## Data sources
1. Services Australia — Annual Reports and customer service statistics
2. Australian Digital Inclusion Index (ADII) — 2023 and 2025 results
3. First Nations Digital Inclusion Index / Snapshot
4. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) — state and territory population estimates
5. Australian geographic boundary data for state and territory mapping
